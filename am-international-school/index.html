The Opportunity

Somalia's Diaspora Wants to Come Home

Over 2 million Somalis live abroad, sending more than $2 billion in remittances home every year. A growing number of these families want to return — to invest, to build, and to raise their children in Somalia. But many hesitate for one reason: education.

There is a well-documented demand for English-medium, internationally recognised schooling in Somalia. Parents want their children to have access to British qualifications — IGCSE, A-Level, and BTEC — that open doors to universities and careers worldwide, while still being rooted in Somali culture, language, and values.

Existing international schools in Mogadishu operate at or near capacity. Parents consistently seek British curriculum options, and demand outweighs available places every academic year.

The Curriculum

British Education, Globally Recognised

AM International School will follow the full British curriculum, from Early Years through to Sixth Form. Students will sit externally examined qualifications through Pearson Edexcel and Cambridge International — the same exam boards used by leading international schools around the world.

Curriculum Overview

From Nursery to Sixth Form

Our academic programme is structured around the English National Curriculum, adapted for an international context and enriched with Somali language, Arabic, and Islamic studies. Students progress through clearly defined key stages, each with its own assessment milestones and qualifications.

  • Early Years & Primary (Ages 5–11): English, Mathematics, Science, Computing, Humanities, Art, PE, Somali, Arabic, Islamic Studies
  • Secondary – Key Stage 3 (Ages 11–14): Broad foundation across all core and creative subjects
  • Key Stage 4 – IGCSE (Ages 14–16): Pearson Edexcel IGCSEs in 8–10 subjects, including English, Maths, Sciences, and electives
  • Sixth Form – A-Level / BTEC (Ages 16–18): AS and A-Level pathways plus Pearson BTEC Level 3 Diplomas for applied, career-focused learning
University-ready. IGCSE and A-Level qualifications are recognised by universities worldwide — including the UK, Europe, North America, the Gulf, and across Africa. BTEC pathways offer direct routes into employment and higher-level apprenticeships.

Facilities & Infrastructure

Built for Learning, Safety, and Community

The campus will be designed to international standards, with separate wings for Primary and Secondary students, secure perimeter fencing, and modern facilities that support both academic and extracurricular excellence.

  • Classrooms: 70+ purpose-built classrooms, equipped with interactive smartboards
  • Science Labs: 6 fully equipped laboratories for Physics, Chemistry, and Biology
  • Computer Labs: 4 ICT suites with 30+ workstations each
  • Library: A two-storey library with digital resources and study spaces
  • Sports Facilities: Football pitch, basketball courts, and an indoor multi-purpose hall
  • Auditorium: 500-seat hall for assemblies, performances, and community events
  • Dining Hall: Modern canteen serving nutritious meals for day and boarding students
  • Health Centre: On-site clinic with a full-time nurse

  • Security: 24/7 guarded perimeter, CCTV, and controlled access points

Capital Expenditure

Investment Breakdown

Building a world-class international school in Somalia requires significant upfront investment. The estimated total capital expenditure is $28–32 million USD, covering land, construction, equipment, and initial operating costs.

Premium international schools in East Africa typically cost $20–35 million to develop, with larger campuses exceeding that range. School construction costs in Somalia are significantly higher than in comparable settings, due to security and logistics requirements.

Revenue Assumptions

How We Reach $14M+ Annual Revenue

Our revenue model is built on a tiered tuition structure that balances accessibility for local families with premium pricing for diaspora and international students. Uniforms and ancillary services add meaningful incremental income on top of tuition.

  • Primary (Nursery–Year 6): 850 students × ~$4,200/year = $3.6M
  • Secondary (Years 7–11, IGCSE): 700 students × ~$6,800/year = $4.8M
  • Sixth Form (Years 12–13, A-Level/BTEC): 450 students × ~$8,500/year = $3.8M
  • Boarding Fees: 250 boarders × ~$3,500/year = $0.9M
  • Uniforms & School Shop: ~1,900 students × ~$550/year = $1.05M
  • Other Income: Registration, transport, summer programmes = $0.5M
Total Annual Revenue at Capacity: ~$14.65M

Development Plan

From Groundbreaking to Grand Opening

The school will be developed in four phases over approximately 24–30 months, allowing for careful planning, quality construction, and a phased enrolment model that stabilises operations before scaling to full capacity.

Development Stages

Four Phases, One Vision

Each phase has clearly defined milestones, budgets, and deliverables. The phased approach minimises risk, allows for course correction, and ensures that students experience a fully prepared campus from day one of each stage.
