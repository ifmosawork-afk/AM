Welcome to Somalia

A Country of Untold Beauty

Somalia is one of the last truly undiscovered destinations on earth. With the longest mainland coastline in Africa, warm turquoise waters, ancient port cities, and a rich nomadic culture stretching back thousands of years, it offers a travel experience unlike anywhere else on the continent.

AM Tourism is your trusted gateway. We handle every detail — from airport pickup and secure transport to experienced local guides, comfortable accommodation, and carefully planned itineraries that show you the real Somalia safely and respectfully.

Whether you're diaspora returning home, an adventurous traveller, or a family looking for something genuinely different, we have a tour built for you.

Our Tours

Four Ways to Experience Somalia

Each of our signature tours is led by experienced local guides and can be booked individually or combined into a multi-day package. All tours include secure transport, refreshments, and full coordination by AM Tourism.

Boat Tours

Boat Tours

Sail along Somalia's stunning Indian Ocean coastline. Crystal-clear waters, untouched beaches, and the chance to see dolphins, sea turtles, and traditional fishing dhows at work.

  • Coastal cruises along the Mogadishu and Marka shoreline
  • Snorkelling & swimming in warm, clear waters
  • Fresh seafood lunch prepared on board
  • Sunset sailing with views of the Indian Ocean
Half or full day
Up to 12 guests
Life jackets & crew
Camel Tours

Camel Tours

Somalia is home to one of the largest camel populations in the world. Ride through traditional nomadic lands and experience a way of life that has barely changed in centuries.

  • Guided camel rides with experienced herders
  • Nomadic camp visit — tea, storytelling, and local customs
  • Fresh camel milk tasting, a Somali tradition
  • Photography stops across open savannah and acacia land
3–5 hours
Small groups
Easy pace
City Tour

City Tour

Explore Mogadishu — a city of resilience, history, and vibrant culture. From ancient architecture to bustling markets, our city tour shows you the capital through the eyes of those who know it best.

  • Historic landmarks and old town architecture
  • Lively markets — spices, fabrics, and local crafts
  • Lido Beach and the waterfront promenade
