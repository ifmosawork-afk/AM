How We Source

Direct from Farmers, Handled with Care

Every spice we export is bought directly from farmers, cooperatives, or trusted local traders. No middlemen, no unnecessary markups. This keeps quality high, prices fair, and communities supported.

Sourcing & Handling

From Farm Gate to Export Container

We handle every stage of the supply chain — sourcing, cleaning, drying, grading, and packaging — following internationally recognised food safety and quality standards. Nothing leaves our facilities until it meets our specification.

  • Direct sourcing: Bought from farmers and cooperatives at fair prices
  • Natural drying: Traditional sun-drying, no artificial heat or chemicals
  • Hand-cleaning: Manual sorting and removal of foreign matter
  • Grading: Graded by size, colour, aroma, and moisture content
  • Moisture control: Tested to ensure optimal shelf life
  • Food-safe packaging: Sealed bags and cartons ready for export
Traceability at every step. Every batch can be traced back to its source — so you always know where your spices come from and how they were handled.
Export Ready

Exporting Internationally, Backed by Full Documentation

We ship spices to buyers across the Middle East, Europe, Asia, and North America. Every shipment leaves with complete export documentation and food safety certifications in place.

Export Ready · Global Shipping

Packed, Documented, Shipped

From our facilities in Kenya and Tanzania, we consolidate, pack, and ship spice shipments to importers, processors, distributors, and private-label brands around the world. Air freight for urgent orders, sea freight for bulk volumes.

  • Packaging formats: 25kg bags, jute sacks, retail pouches, private label
  • Shipping: Sea freight (bulk) and air freight (premium/urgent orders)
  • Documentation: Phytosanitary certificate, Certificate of Origin, quality analysis
  • Compliance: HACCP-aligned handling, food-safe packaging, traceable batches
  • Markets served: Middle East, Europe, Asia, North America, and beyond
Export-ready from day one. Whether you're importing a single pallet or a full container, we handle the paperwork, the packing, and the shipping — you just receive the delivery.
