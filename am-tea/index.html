Kenyan Tea Heritage

Why Kenya Produces Exceptional Tea

Kenya is one of the world's largest tea exporters and is internationally renowned for producing teas of exceptional brightness, body, and flavour. The country's high-altitude estates — sitting between 1,500 and 2,700 metres above sea level — combined with the volcanic soil, equatorial climate, and two rainy seasons per year, create ideal conditions for the tea bush.

The result is a tea that is bold, brisk, and rich in colour — highly prized by blenders around the world and used to add strength and brightness to premium blends.

Our Portfolio

The Four Core Grades We Export

We source our teas through Kenya's internationally respected auction system and through direct relationships with leading estates. Our core export range covers four key grades — each with distinct characteristics suited to different markets and blends.

BP1
Broken Pekoe 1

A well-balanced broken leaf grade offering a smooth, full-bodied cup with bright colour and consistent quality. BP1 is a benchmark grade for the Kenyan market and widely used in premium consumer blends.

PF1
Pekoe Fanning 1

High-quality fannings with excellent liquor characteristics. PF1 is widely used in premium tea blends and tea bags, delivering a bright, brisk cup with strong colour.

PD
Pekoe Dust

Fine particle grade ideal for tea bags and bulk blends. PD is known for its bold flavour, rich colour, and quick brewing — the workhorse grade for many fast-moving consumer products.

FNGS
Standard Fannings

Small leaf particles that brew quickly and deliver a strong, brisk cup. Standard fannings are ideal for everyday consumption and bulk blends at scale.
