Our Philosophy

Protecting Oceans. Supporting Families.

AM Sea Foods was founded on a simple principle: the ocean can feed the world for generations only if we treat it with respect. We refuse to participate in destructive industrial fishing. Every fish we export is caught naturally, by hand, from small boats — the way coastal communities have fished for centuries.

Small-Boat Fishing Only

We work exclusively with traditional small-scale fishermen using hand lines, traps, and small dhows — never industrial trawlers.

Coral & Reef Protection

We prohibit all fishing methods that damage coral reefs, seagrass beds, or marine nurseries. No dynamite. No bottom trawling. No nets that scrape the seabed.

Organic & Natural

Our seafood is caught in the wild and handled without chemical preservatives, growth hormones, or artificial processing.

Fair Direct Trade

Fishermen are paid fairly and immediately. No middlemen. No exploitation. Profits stay in the coastal communities.

Seasonal & Sustainable

We respect breeding seasons and size limits, ensuring fish populations remain healthy for future generations.

Long-Term Partnerships

We build lasting relationships with fishing families — not one-time transactions — so communities can plan for their future.

Our Products

Premium Seafood from the Coast

We handle a wide range of marine species — from sea cucumber and shellfish to tuna and reef fish. Each product is sourced, handled, and preserved according to international food safety standards.

Product Focus

Sea Cucumber

Sea cucumber (beche-de-mer) is one of the most valuable marine exports from the East African coast. Highly prized in Asian markets for its culinary and medicinal properties, it is a luxury delicacy used in soups, tonics, and traditional remedies.

We have extensive experience harvesting, processing, and exporting sea cucumber from the Kenyan and Somali coasts. Our product is sun-dried and hand-processed to preserve texture and nutritional quality.

  • Forms available: Dried (traditional beche-de-mer), frozen
  • Species: Various local varieties including Holothuria and Actinopyga
  • Export markets: China, Hong Kong, Singapore, Malaysia, Vietnam
  • Handling: Traditional cleaning, boiling, and sun-drying methods

We work closely with coastal communities who harvest sea cucumber responsibly by hand, protecting reef ecosystems and maintaining sustainable population levels.

Product Focus

Crabs & Lobsters

The East African coast is home to some of the finest crustaceans in the world. Our crabs and lobsters are caught by hand — by small-boat fishermen using traps and manual diving — never by drag nets or destructive gear.

We supply premium live, fresh, and frozen crabs and lobsters to luxury hotels, restaurants, and seafood importers worldwide.

  • Crabs: Mud crabs, blue swimmers, mangrove crabs
  • Lobsters: Spiny lobsters, rock lobsters, slipper lobsters
  • Forms available: Live, fresh-chilled, whole-frozen, lobster tails
  • Handling: Immediate ice or cold-water immersion on the boat to preserve freshness

We insist on minimum size limits for every crustacean we buy — keeping undersized juveniles in the ocean so populations can regenerate naturally.

Underexploited Resource · Koyoma

Puffer Fish

The waters off Koyoma are home to substantial puffer fish populations that remain largely unexploited. Puffer fish is a highly regulated, high-value delicacy — particularly in Japan (fugu), South Korea, and select fine-dining markets worldwide — and demand consistently exceeds supply.

We are developing a controlled, fully compliant puffer fish export operation from the Koyoma coast, working only with trained handlers and licensed buyers. This creates new income opportunities for local fishermen while opening a premium market niche for our export partners.

  • Species: Local puffer fish varieties from the Koyoma coast
  • Forms available: Frozen whole, skinless fillets (per buyer specification)
  • Target markets: Japan, South Korea, high-end Asian restaurants
  • Handling: Strictly trained handlers; toxin-safe processing protocols
⚠️ Important — Safety & Certification Requirements:
Puffer fish contains tetrodotoxin and is highly regulated in most importing countries. All buyers must hold a valid import licence and demonstrate certified handling capability for puffer fish under their country's food safety regulations (e.g. Japan's fugu licensing system, South Korea's puffer fish certification, or equivalent). We will only supply buyers who can present the required certificates and documentation before shipment is confirmed. Safety — for the end consumer and for our team — is non-negotiable.
