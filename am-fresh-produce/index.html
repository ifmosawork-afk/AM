Kenyan Fresh Produce

Every Fruit. Every Season. From Kenya.

Kenya is one of the most biodiverse fruit-producing nations in Africa. From the coastal tropics of Mombasa, Kilifi, and Lamu — where tropical fruits thrive year-round — to the highland plateaus around Mount Kenya and the Rift Valley, where temperate and subtropical varieties flourish, Kenya delivers fruit across the full spectrum.

Because of our equatorial climate and two rainy seasons each year, we are able to harvest a continuous supply of fruits — giving buyers the reliability and variety they need for year-round programs. Working with smallholder farmers and cooperatives across the country, we consolidate, inspect, and export the very best of Kenya's harvest.

How We Grow

Grown Naturally, Following Strict Protocols

Every fruit we export — and every potato, vegetable, and tuber alongside it — is grown following internationally recognised agricultural guidelines. We work only with farmers who commit to natural, sustainable practices on their land.

Naturally Grown · Following Global Protocols

Everything Grows Naturally

Our partner farmers follow strict agricultural guidelines covering planting, irrigation, soil health, and harvesting. No shortcuts. No destructive practices. Every crop is grown the natural way — with the soil, the sun, and the rain.

The potatoes shown above are a perfect example: grown in Kenya's highland regions where the volcanic soil and cool climate produce firm, flavourful tubers. From planting to harvesting to packing, every stage follows protocols that meet international food safety and export standards.

  • Natural cultivation: No growth hormones, no artificial ripening
  • Responsible irrigation: Water-efficient drip and rain-fed systems
  • Soil health: Crop rotation, composting, and organic matter management
  • Integrated pest management: Minimising chemical use, protecting pollinators
  • Hand harvesting: Selective picking at peak ripeness for better quality
  • Post-harvest handling: Cool-chain, hygiene-controlled, and traceable
Every farm. Every crop. Every time. We personally visit, verify, and audit every farm we work with — so you know exactly where your food comes from.
Produce Spotlight

Pineapples — Kenya's Tropical Signature

Kenyan pineapples are one of the country's most celebrated fruit exports. Grown in the coastal belt and irrigated by clean tropical rainfall, they are known for their exceptional sweetness, low acidity, and golden flesh.

Deep Dive · Pineapples

Why Kenyan Pineapples Stand Out

Kenya's coastal pineapple farms benefit from year-round warm temperatures, rich tropical soils, and just the right balance of sun and rainfall. The result is a fruit with a deeper golden colour, higher natural sugar content, and a flavour that consistently outperforms other origins in blind taste tests.

We source our pineapples directly from partner farms in Kilifi, Malindi, and the wider coastal belt. Every pineapple is hand-selected at peak ripeness and packed in export-grade cartons designed to protect the fruit through long-haul shipping.

  • Variety: Smooth Cayenne and MD-2 (Sweet Gold)
  • Sizes available: Count 6, 8, 10, 12, 14 per carton
  • Brix level: 12–16° — naturally sweet, no added sugar
  • Forms: Fresh whole, fresh-cut chunks, frozen slices, juice concentrate
  • Packaging: Export cartons or private-label retail packs
Fruit that sells itself. Once your customers taste a Kenyan pineapple, they come back for more. Pineapple is one of our most reordered products from wholesale and retail buyers around the world.

Export Ready · Tamarind

Tamarind — The Tangy Export Favourite

Tamarind is one of East Africa's most versatile export fruits. Prized for its tangy, sweet-and-sour flavour, it is used in cuisines across the Middle East, South Asia, Latin America, and beyond — in chutneys, sauces, beverages, candies, and traditional remedies.

We source tamarind from coastal and lowland farmers who harvest it naturally, sun-dry it using traditional methods, and hand-clean and grade it for export. Tamarind's natural acidity and low moisture content make it an excellent long-shelf-life product for international buyers.

  • Forms: Whole pods, shelled, seedless block, paste, and powder
  • Packaging: Bulk 25kg cartons, retail pouches, or private label
  • Shelf life: 18–24 months when stored cool and dry
  • Certifications: Phytosanitary, Certificate of Origin, HACCP-compliant handling
  • Export markets: Middle East, South Asia, Europe, North America
Export-ready from day one. Our tamarind ships with full documentation, quality certificates, and food-safe packaging — ready for your distribution or processing network.
